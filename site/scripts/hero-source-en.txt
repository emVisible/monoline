Most creatures of the deep make their own light. This is not reflected sunlight; it is a chemical reaction inside the body. When luciferin meets oxygen, a photon is released. About 76% of deep-sea animals glow. Three groups account for most of them: lanternfish, hatchetfish, and the vampire squid. Blue-green light travels furthest through water, so most signals use it. Some anglerfish can see red, and almost nothing else down there can. Bioluminescence is the most common form of communication on Earth.
